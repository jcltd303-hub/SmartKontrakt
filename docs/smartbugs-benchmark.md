## What this does and does not say

- These are 2018-era contracts on Solidity 0.4.x/0.5.x. A detector that needs a modern construct (a Uniswap v4 hook, an EIP-7702 guard, a `latestRoundData` feed) cannot fire here; it is absent from the table rather than scored as a miss.
- The dataset annotates *lines*; this analyzer reports *sites*. The join would measure whitespace normalisation, not detection, so scoring is file-level per category.
- A category with zero expected contracts (`other`, mostly) has no recall to measure and shows 0.
